    About the project

    Floods, gas leaks and unsafe movement in restricted areas often give early, measurable signs before they become emergencies. This project uses several low-cost sensors connected to an 8051 microcontroller to watch for those signs and raise a local warning with LEDs and a buzzer.

    Problem statement

    Single-purpose alarms watch for one hazard at a time and many low-resource settings have no early warning at all. The aim is a single, affordable multi-sensor unit that checks water level, gas concentration, human presence and obstacles against programmed thresholds and gives a clear, graded local alert (normal, caution, danger).

    This web page is a simulation of that alert logic for demonstration and teaching. It does not read live hardware unless the experimental USB serial input is used.

    Sensors

    Gas sensor

    A heated sensing element changes its resistance when combustible or harmful gases are present. The change is read as a gas level and compared with a threshold.

    Role: gas-leak detection (proposed).

    Ultrasonic sensor

    Sends a short ultrasonic pulse and times its echo. Distance = speed of sound × echo time ÷ 2.

    Role: distance to the water surface for flood monitoring.

    PIR sensor

    A pyroelectric element senses changes in infrared radiation from warm bodies. It reacts to movement, not to a person standing still.

    Role: human presence detection.

    IR obstacle sensor

    An infrared LED shines forward and a photodiode looks for light reflected back. A strong reflection means an object is close.

    Role: obstacle at a blocked point.

    Touch sensor

    A touch pad detects the change caused by a finger contact and gives a digital output.

    Role: manual acknowledge to silence the buzzer (proposed).

    Limitations

    • This page is a simulation; values come from on-screen controls.
    • Thresholds are set by hand and the sensors are not calibrated.
    • Gas sensors need a warm-up period and can respond to more than one gas.
    • Ultrasonic readings can be disturbed by waves, foam and temperature changes.
    • The PIR sensor detects motion only, so a still person may be missed.
    • The IR obstacle sensor has a short range and depends on surface colour and ambient light.
    • Alerts are local only: LEDs and a buzzer at the device.
    • The 8051 has limited memory and processing capacity.
    • The system has not been field-tested and is not a certified warning system.

    Future scope

    • Wireless communication modules to send readings from the device.
    • Cloud storage and a remote dashboard for long-term records.
    • Mobile app and SMS alerts to people away from the device.
    • More sensors such as rain, temperature, smoke and vibration.
    • Proper calibration of every sensor against reference instruments.
    • Battery or solar backup for operation during power cuts.
    • Analysis of recorded data to study trends before hazards develop.
